Group the scripts/ directory by purpose

`scripts/` had 25 loose files at the top level next to its subdirectories. They are now grouped by why a script is run: `scripts/build/` (opam switch, pinned packages, Go helper), `scripts/lint/` (the repository lint gates), `scripts/genesis/` (genesis and ledger generation), and `scripts/tests/` (test runners and test-fixture regeneration). The two hard-fork dry-run ledger scripts joined `scripts/hardfork/`, and `mina.service` joined `scripts/debian/`, which is the packaging that ships it.

The Rosetta test scripts were split between `scripts/tests/` and `scripts/rosetta/`; they are now all under `scripts/tests/rosetta/`, and `scripts/rosetta/` is gone. `scripts/export-git-env-vars.sh` stays at the top level: it belongs to no single group and nearly everything sources it.

Only the paths changed, not the behaviour of any script. `scripts/README.md` records what belongs where. Anything referring to a script by its old path needs updating; every reference inside this repository already has been.
