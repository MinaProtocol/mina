Prove zkApp transactions with fewer SNARK proofs

Up to six consecutive account updates that are authorized by signatures or need no authorization are now proved together in a single SNARK proof, where previously they were proved two at a time and the proofs then merged. For example, a zkApp payment from one account to three others now needs one proof instead of five, reducing the work SNARK workers do for such transactions. Which zkApp transactions are valid is unchanged. This changes the transaction SNARK verification key, so it takes effect at a hard fork.
