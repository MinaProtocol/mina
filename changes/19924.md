Win more slots when a block producer cannot check every delegator in time

When a block producer has too many delegators to check all of them before a slot, it now checks the delegators with the most stake first, since they are the most likely to win. In a test with 100,000 delegators that could not all be checked in time, this raised the share of slots won from 77% to 96% of the maximum possible. A node running several block producer keys now also picks the best winner across all of its keys, instead of the first key that won.
