archive_hardfork_toolbox: `populate-genesis-accounts` is about 50–90 times faster

The command writes the genesis ledger with set-based statements, a chunk of accounts per transaction, instead of one account at a time. On the mesa fork genesis it takes 70 s for devnet (92,191 accounts) and 136 s for mainnet (288,965 accounts), instead of about 65 minutes and 3.5 hours. The rows are the same. The default of `--chunks-length` is now 2000.
