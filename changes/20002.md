Load the genesis ledger once when the archive starts with --legacy-genesis-accounts

With `--config-file` and `--legacy-genesis-accounts`, the archive node loaded the config's genesis ledger twice at startup: once to store the genesis block and again to write its accounts to `accounts_accessed`. It now loads the ledger once, which makes startup faster on large ledgers. The archive still stops when the stored genesis block does not match `--config-file`.
