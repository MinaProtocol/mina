archive_hardfork_toolbox: `populate-genesis-accounts` writes `genesis_accounts`

The command writes the genesis ledger of a runtime config to the `genesis_accounts` table, at the height of the config's genesis block, instead of to `accounts_accessed` of the genesis block. It takes seconds instead of hours on a mainnet ledger. The flags are the same; the default of `--chunks-length` is now 5000.
