### Added

- The archive node now reports `Mina_Archive_ingest_duration_ms`, a Prometheus
  histogram of how long it held each block ingest request, labelled by the
  source that sent the block (`diff` for the daemon feed, `precomputed` or
  `extensional` for a block pushed by a client).

  The archive holds the sender's call until the block is written, and a daemon
  that waits longer than the RPC heartbeat timeout gives up and drops the
  block. This metric is the span the sender waits for, so it shows that
  situation approaching instead of only its result: a gap in the archive.

  It needs `--metrics-port`. Without that flag the archive behaves exactly as
  before.

### Changed

- With `--metrics-port` set, the archive's metrics endpoint now starts
  answering before the genesis ledger is imported rather than after it.
