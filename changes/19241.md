Remove the daemon-side trust system; peer bans now live in the libp2p helper

The daemon no longer keeps per-peer trust scores in a local database. Peers
that commit a provable offense (invalid proof, bad hash, disconnected chain,
and similar) are banned immediately, and the libp2p helper computes the ban
duration, escalating it for repeat offenders up to 30 days. Automatic bans are
in-memory only and no longer survive a restart; manual bans are persisted by
the helper in `libp2p_banlist.json` and are indefinite until removed. Old
`<config-dir>/trust` directories are no longer used and can be deleted.

The CLI commands `get-trust-status`, `get-trust-status-all` and
`reset-trust-status` are removed. In GraphQL, `trustStatus`, `trustStatusAll`,
`resetTrustStatus`, `setConnectionGatingConfig` and the ITN `updateGating`
mutation are removed; new queries `bans` and `trustedPeers` and new mutations
`banPeer`, `unbanPeer`, `addTrustedPeer`, `removeTrustedPeer` and `setIsolate`
replace them. The `connectionGatingConfig` query now reports only `isolate`.
The `banned_peers` Prometheus gauge is still exported, now without the
`Trust_system` subsystem prefix.
