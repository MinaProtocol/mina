Settle prefork pending blocks in the archive node with --settle-prefork-pending-blocks

With `--settle-prefork-pending-blocks` (or `MINA_ARCHIVE_SETTLE_PREFORK_PENDING_BLOCKS`) and a hard fork's `--config-file`, the archive node settles the hard fork boundary once at startup, after the config's genesis is stored: the chain to the fork block becomes canonical, the other blocks of the ended chain are orphaned. A boundary that cannot be settled yet (the fork block or its ancestry is missing) is logged, and settled later with the hardfork toolbox. Without the flag the archive node does not settle the boundary.

The hardfork toolbox `convert-chain-to-canonical` command now settles the hard fork boundary of the newest fork genesis, with the same logic as the archive node. It refuses when the fork genesis is not canonical, and fails when the boundary cannot be settled. Its `--target-block-hash`, `--fork-height`, `--protocol-version`, `--stop-at-slot` and `--json` flags were removed. Only `--dry-run` remains.
