Settle the hard fork boundary in the archive node with --hardfork-handover

With `--hardfork-handover` and a `--config-file`, the archive node settles the hard fork boundary once at startup, after the genesis ledger is written. With `--hardfork-handover` and no `--config-file`, it settles the boundary when the hard fork genesis block is stored. Without the flag the archive node does not settle the boundary, so behaviour is unchanged.
