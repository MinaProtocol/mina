Add a bootstrap-status command to mina-graphql-client

`mina-graphql-client bootstrap-status` prints the result of the node's `bootstrapStatus` GraphQL query as JSON. The output covers the node's current step on its way to the network's chain, its bootstrap attempts, and the progress of each ledger it syncs from peers. Scripts can poll it to follow a bootstrap, or to wait until the node is `PARTICIPATING`, without writing their own GraphQL query.
