Fix the daemon crashing at startup when the seed peer list cannot be downloaded

The Mainnet and Devnet configurations make the daemon download its seed peer list from `bootnodes.minaprotocol.com`, even when peers are given with `--peer-list-file` or `--peer`. If that host could not be reached, the daemon stopped at startup with a `connection attempt timeout` error. The daemon now logs a warning and continues with the peers it already has.
