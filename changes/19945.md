Reject blocks whose body does not match their header

A peer could send a copy of a new block with its header intact but a different body. A node that received this copy first ignored the real block, did not forward it, and only discovered the copy was invalid later, delaying the real block until it arrived again or was caught up. Nodes now check a block's body against its header as soon as it arrives, reject a mismatched copy and penalise the peer that sent it, and keep accepting the real block.
