Fix RPC requests failing with "only wrote 0 out of N bytes: stream reset"

Nodes kept every RPC stream a peer had opened to them for 30 seconds after the request was answered. A peer that sent many requests in a short time, such as a node syncing ledgers during bootstrap, had its new requests refused once it held more than about 64, and they failed with this error. Streams are now released as soon as each request is answered, so those requests succeed and ledger sync no longer stalls on failed batches.
