Fix nodes getting stuck in bootstrap

A long-standing and common cause of nodes stalling in bootstrap has been fixed. If the network's snarked ledger changed while a starting node was downloading it, the download could stall, often for minutes and sometimes indefinitely. The node stayed in `Bootstrap` and needed restarting. This happened most on slow or busy connections, where the download takes longest. Nodes now switch to the new ledger straight away and finish bootstrapping in their usual time.
