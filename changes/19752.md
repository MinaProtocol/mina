Archive no longer writes the genesis ledger to `accounts_accessed` at start-up

With `--config-file`, the archive wrote every genesis ledger account to `accounts_accessed` of the genesis block before it accepted blocks: about 65 minutes on devnet and 3.5 hours on mainnet at the mesa hard fork. It now adds only the genesis block, starts to accept blocks, and writes the ledger to `genesis_accounts` in the background. `--chunks-length` now sets the chunk size of that write (default 5000).
