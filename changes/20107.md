Make block proofs attest to how pending coinbases are tracked

A block's proof previously left the bookkeeping of pending coinbases to the prover, so a chain could satisfy the proof while dropping, reordering or misattributing coinbases that full validation would reject. Blocks now record the position of the pending coinbase they are filling, exposed over GraphQL as `BlockchainState.pendingCoinbaseStackIndex`, and the block proof checks every pending coinbase update against it. This is a hard-fork change: block proofs, the genesis state hash and the staged ledger hash of every block change.

The `--old-stack` flag of `mina ledger test apply` is removed.
