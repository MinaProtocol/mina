Settle pending archive blocks after a hard fork

After a hard fork the archive left the last blocks before the fork pending for ever, and could keep blocks it had made canonical past the fork point. `mina-archive run --settle-pending-blocks` now settles them before the archive accepts blocks: the chain to the fork block becomes canonical and the rest of the ended chain is orphaned, and pending blocks at heights that already have a canonical block are orphaned. `mina-archive settle-pending-blocks [--dry-run]` does the same on demand.
