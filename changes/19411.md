Fix peer discovery over the DHT, which stopped working shortly after a node started

Nodes now keep finding new peers for as long as they run, instead of only during the first moments after startup. Nodes that cannot be reached from the outside gain the most: on mainnet such a node ran at roughly two thirds of the peer count of a reachable one. If your node sits behind NAT without a forwarded port, expect it to hold more peers than before. Fixes #19385.
