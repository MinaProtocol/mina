Speed up reading zkApp accounts

Reading a zkApp account with a verification key no longer computes the key's hash unless it is needed, which made each such read about 25 times cheaper. Nodes on networks with many zkApp accounts spend less time on ledger reads and GraphQL account queries, and computing the stake distribution at an epoch boundary is several times faster (on a devnet epoch ledger, 7.4–9.0 s down to 0.8 s).
