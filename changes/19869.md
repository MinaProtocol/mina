Add a bootstrapStatus GraphQL query

The new `bootstrapStatus` query reports what a node is doing on its way to the network's chain: the step it is in (`phase`, such as `SYNCING_ROOT_LEDGER` or `SYNCING_EPOCH_LEDGERS`) and when that step began, how many attempts the current bootstrap has made and why the last one failed, and the progress of each ledger it syncs from peers (`ledgerSyncs`), including how many of the ledger's accounts it already holds (`accountsSettled` out of `numAccounts`) and when a peer last sent useful data (`lastAcceptedAnswerAt`). Operators can poll it to follow a slow bootstrap, or to tell a stalled ledger sync apart from one that is still making progress.
